Maximally fused kernel vs the two-kernel fused path
==================================================
RTX 4090. The fully fused kernel (src/logmel/triton_fused_full.py) takes the raw complex
FFT output and does magnitude, mel projection, log, and the per-utterance max
reduction (via a GPU atomic) in one kernel. The two-kernel fused path starts from
the power spectrum and leaves the max as a separate reduction. Speedups are
reference / kernel.

Reproduce: PYTHONPATH=src python bench/compare_full.py

  dtype  cell     fused      full
  fp16   b1  1s   1.08x      1.37x
  fp16   b8 10s   1.20x      1.10x
  fp16   b32 30s  1.06x      1.22x
  fp32   b1  1s   0.89x      0.52x
  fp32   b8 10s   1.04x      0.47x
  fp32   b32 30s  0.98x      0.59x

Correctness: matches the reference exactly enough (fp32 max relative error
5e-04, fp16 1.6e-03).

Reading: folding in the magnitude and the max reduction raises the fp16 win, most
on short clips (1.08x to 1.37x) and at large batch (1.06x to 1.22x),
because it removes the materialized power spectrum and the standalone amax. The
fp32 path regresses, because its IEEE matmul is slow and the extra STFT handling
is pure overhead there. fp32 was never where this fusion helps, so the fully
fused kernel is an fp16 win.
